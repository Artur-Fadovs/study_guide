# Solving by Factorising

A quadratic equation can sometimes be solved by factorising it into two linear expressions.

For example:

$$
x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0
$$

can be factorised as:

$$
(x + 2)(x + 3) = 0
$$

Therefore:

$$
x = -2
$$

or

$$
x = -3
$$
